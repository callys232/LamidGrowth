[0037] 3. Continuous Intelligence, Persistent Progression & Authority
[0038] Continuous Intelligence may refresh permitted context and analysis as inputs, workflows, connected systems, events, and outcomes change. It can detect what changed, what improved, what became constrained, what became possible, and what deserves attention next. Continuous Intelligence does not itself grant execution authority.
[0039] Persistent Progression means permitted context, intelligence, and already-authorized work may continue advancing across time rather than freezing between interactions. This can include synchronization, recalculation, monitoring, scheduled analysis, status advancement, pattern detection, and workflow steps already authorized within defined scope.
[0040] Three progression lanes define what “progress continues” actually means:
[0041] Context progresses — permitted inputs, connected-state changes, evidence, workflow state, and relevant conditions can update the operating context.
[0042] Intelligence progresses — LAMID ONE can reassess, recalculate, compare, forecast, detect patterns, identify constraints or opportunities, and refresh recommendations as permitted context changes.
[0043] Authorized work progresses — scheduled routines, integrations, calculations, monitoring, status transitions, and workflow steps may continue only where that exact work was already authorized within a defined scope.
[0044] Absence never creates authority. LAMID ONE does not infer a new permission, widen a scope, approve a consequential decision, or convert observation into execution because a user is away.
[0045] All progression is explicitly permissioned, scoped, revocable, traceable, interruptible, and non-expanding.
[0046] No permission expands itself.
[0047] Permission to observe does not become permission to act.
[0048] Permission to analyze does not become permission to approve.
[0049] Permission for one workflow does not authorize another.
[0050] Authorized humans can inspect, redirect, pause, revoke, and override progression.
[0051] Where technically possible and safe, reversible actions should support rollback, restoration, or compensating action.
[0052] Irreversible or materially consequential actions pause for human approval unless that exact authority was explicitly delegated in advance under policy.
[0053] Authority bands: Observe & Update -> Progress Within Authorization -> Consequential Commitment. A lower band never silently becomes a higher band.
[0054] Return-state priority: what changed, what improved, what moved forward, what became more important, what assumption changed, what opportunity appeared, what stalled, what was paused or overridden, and what needs human judgment next. Routine progression is summarized calmly rather than converted into constant alerts.
